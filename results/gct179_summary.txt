0. Correction: the earlier "floor 6e-4" is a chart artifact
   The iso chart (iso.py; cloud modes iso/cont/mix-iso) does not scale-normalise. Its basis vectors E_i + sum c F_j
   can be short, so a small f does not mean the forms are small on unit vectors.
   Local test (seed 11, 331 iso restarts at d=7, best chart f = 8.05e-4 ... 2.1e-3). For the top 5 minima:
     - rms of E1..E4 on random unit vectors of the orthonormalised 7-space: E1, E2 about 1e-13 (zero by construction),
       E3 = 0.06-0.09, E4 = 0.015. The residual sits in E3 (dominant) and E4.
     - Re-polishing by LM in an orthonormal chart centred at the minimum converges (|grad| ~1e-9, J well conditioned,
       smallest singular values 0.3-0.7) to genuine non-degenerate local minima with f = 0.097, 0.17, 0.21, 0.29, 0.47.
     - These minima are NOT "6-dim solution + 1 vector". The best hyperplane inside each 7-space (LM over G(6,7)) has
       f = 0.17-0.66. The nearest exact 6-dim solution is at max principal angle 0.65-0.89 rad (some starts did not
       converge to any 6-solution: f 3e-3 to 2e-2). The 6-solutions that were reached have full support (20 entries)
       and are not row/col crosses.
   The gen chart ([I X]U with unitary U) and the centred charts in ext6.py, seeds.py and sym.py evaluate the forms on
   vectors of norm >= 1, so their f is an honest upper bound. All d=7 numbers below are honest, except where marked.

1b. The universal obstruction also holds at RANDOM generic 6-solutions (new; numerical)
   10 random full-support 6-solutions came from LM (seed 5). Their tangent dims of 7-10 put them on several different
   components, and none is a cross, F_b, W6 or g180. For every one of them:
     - the first-order extension space is T(H) = H + <w>, 1-dim modulo H
       (singular-value floor 1e-7 against a gap of 2e-3);
     - all mixed terms of E_k(h + s w) vanish to within accuracy, so E_k is constant on the coset w + H;
     - the profile is exactly per(J + mu w) = 120 (1+mu)^3 (1-3 mu) after rescaling, i.e. in the problem's
       normalisation E3^2/(E2 E4) = 16/3 and E2^2/E4 = -40 (matched to about 1e-5 relative);
     - w = J - 20 e_rc mod H for ONE distinguished cell (r,c). After Gauss-Newton polishing (|resid| 1e-12) the
       first-order residual of J - 20 e_rc is 1.6e-6 relative, against 1e-2 for every other cell. Equivalently, the
       restriction of H to the 3x4 minor that avoids row r and column c kills the 3x4 forms E1, E2, E3: about 1e-5
       relative, against O(1) for other minors. Note per(J + mu(J - 20 e_rc)) = 120(1+mu)^3(1-3mu) identically.
   The same holds for the 12 symmetric 6-solutions found by sym.py (seed 4; classes such as (01)|(01), e|(0123),
   (012)|(01)(23)). After polishing, J - 20 e_rc passes the first-order test at 1e-6 to 1e-5 relative, against 5e-3 to
   2e-2 for the other cells. Ties occur only where two cells are equivalent mod H. Total: 22 numerical 6-solutions plus
   the 4 exact families, no exception.
   So the obstruction attacker A found in the four rigid families appears to hold for EVERY 6-solution:
   "each 6-solution H has a cell (r,c) with H|minor(r,c) a 3x4 solution, and T(H) = H + <J - 20 e_rc>", where
   E2(J - 20 e_rc) != 0. If T(H) is 7-dim for every hyperplane H of a putative 7-dim L, then L = T(H) contains
   J - 20 e_rc, which fails E2, so L cannot exist. The gap: the hyperplanes of a putative L are special 6-solutions,
   and T(H) could be larger there. Suggested lemma for the provers: show that a 6-solution always has such a cell.

3. Landscape verdict
   d=6: exact solutions (f ~ 1e-18) from 10-50% of starts under every method.
   d=7: about 5000 random restarts, about 800 seeded grows, and 164 symmetric-ansatz runs. Not one reached an honest
        residual below about 0.09. The best minima from unrelated methods (random iso re-polished 0.097, F_b grows 0.091,
        ext6 0.113, sym 0.113) cluster at the same level, about 0.1. They are non-degenerate local minima: J has full
        rank and a smallest singular value of about 0.3. There is no slowly decreasing tail.
   This is consistent with nonexistence of a 7-dim complex L, but it is not a proof. An isolated 7-solution with a tiny
   basin cannot be excluded numerically.
