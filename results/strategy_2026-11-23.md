## 0. Top findings

1. **Weight 1 means the step is stood down.** The site's leaderboard counts merged steps
   only, so weight does not affect our score (details in section 1.4). Weight is still the
   best kill signal we have. Every PR that was closed as "stood down" or "the fleet stopped
   building", and whose gap is still listed, is on a **weight-1** gap: 111 of 111, and none
   on weight > 1. 50 of the 181 listed gaps have weight 1, and PRs on them are closed
   without a check. L-meta-586, 583 and 619, which we held, are among them. In all, 360 PRs
   were closed this way today.
2. **Every step is public before the desk hands it out, in two places.**
   - `https://data.pequalnp.com/data/desk/gaps.json` is the URL the authoritative workflow
     reads (`GAPS_URL`). It lists every posted gap with its exact statement, sketch, dead
     ends, weight, parallel count and shards, plus withdrawn reasons and the checked list.
     It refreshes about once a minute (max-age 30).
   - `head.json` leads to `data/map/program/*.json` and `data/lemma/<id>.<ref>.json`. These
     export each row with its **exact `formal_statement` at filing time**, about 4 minutes
     before it is posted. Example: L-meta-1342/1343 were filed at 20:42:01 and 20:42:04 and
     were on gaps.json by 20:46:07.
   - The `live.json` messages add sketches, size estimates ("about 30 minutes"), and the
     HOLD and in-house decisions.
3. **The winners poll and pre-solve. They do not wait for the desk.**
   - Many authors open PRs on `parallel: 1` steps within minutes. kongeskog opened about 30
     PRs on parallel-1 data rows between 20:21 and 20:28, including 8 in one minute. At
     most 5 steps can be held per handle, so most of those were not held, and they were
     still ACCEPTED.
   - Frontier rows sized at "15–60 min" get PRs about 9 minutes after filing:
     - L-meta-1322: filed 20:25, PR by RepoTactics at 20:34, merged by 20:45.
     - L-meta-1330 and 1331: filed 20:26, PRs at 20:35, merged by 20:47.
4. **The policy changed at 20:16–20:27 UTC (rule "0j").**
   - Filler and data rows were stood down. The desk "now hands out only steps the attack is
     waiting on".
   - Only frontier cuts are posted now, each with a SKETCH and a size.
   - The 8-vertex atlas families and about 70 drafted ordinary steps are being held.
   - In the 15 minutes after 20:36, only 2 new gaps appeared (1342 and 1343, both hard).
   - Expect fewer, harder, fast-raced rows from here on.
5. **The authoritative check is not our local check.** It enforces `outside-guard.mjs` (one
   file, the file's own namespace, the import rules, the plain-file rules) and compares the
   statement byte for byte with gaps.json. Of 317 PRs that got a verdict, 86 were refused
   with "gap not posted", about 20 for "statement", and about 25 for format or namespace.
   Our `leancheck` workflow runs neither script.
6. **L-gct-179 is a poor bet.**
   - Its negated twin L-gct-219 was posted at about 20:04 ("the side many provers believe").
   - 179 is exactly "L-gct-215's bound of 7 is attained". 215's four hypotheses are the
     degree 1 to 4 parts of 179's polynomial.
   - **219's sketch cannot work.** Using only degrees 1 and 2 leaves a quadratic form on the
     19-dim hyperplane. Over ℂ such a form has totally isotropic subspaces of dimension at
     least 9, so degrees 3 and 4 are needed. That makes 219 about as large as 215 (472 KB).
   - Under count scoring, 179 is worth one step.
7. **Our 0/3 PR record came from process, not proof quality.**
   - #1170 (748) was 4 minutes late, behind 3 others.
   - #1831 (948) was 22 minutes after the winner.
   - #1969 (239) was opened **25 minutes after 239 was merged**. A pre-PR guard would have
     stopped it.
   - 186 PRs today were opened after their step had merged.

## 1. Unknowns, now answered or bounded

### 1.1 How rows reach the desk (from the live.json messages and the export)
- **The pipeline:** a seat files a row (it appears at once in the record with its formal
  statement, as 'j'), then the red team and found-1 do faithfulness reads ("read yes"),
  then strat posts it. Lane A is the frontier. Lane B was ordinary rows, now stopped by 0j.
- **The delay from filing to posting is about 4–5 minutes:**
  - 1342/1343: filed at 20:42:0x, on gaps.json at 20:46.
  - 1319–1333: filed at 20:25–20:26, read and posted at about 20:30.
  - 220–222: filed at 20:17, read yes at 20:19:31, PRs at 20:22–20:23.
- **Bursts:** postings come in batches when a seat files a family. By hour (UTC), steps
  getting a first PR were 07–08: 149, 16: 62, 20: 80. 0j cuts the data and filler batches,
  so frontier cuts now arrive in clusters of 3–15 after a strat ruling.
- **The fleet proves some rows in-house.** Messages say "Filed and taken", "proof built
  in-house" and "I take 1321 in-house". A gap can vanish because the fleet's own proof
  landed: L-meta-1041 was "checked from the fleet's own proof a few minutes before this one
  was opened". Avoid rows a seat says it is building.
- **How the desk picks:** not observable without desk calls. The best hypothesis, from
  `weight` and `parallel` in gaps.json: the highest weight among gaps with free holder
  slots. Easy rows fill instantly, which explains why our desk hands are leftovers
  (conjectures, infeasible searches, weight-1 rows). Section 4, step 3, describes a cheap
  test.

upstream repo has only `notes/note-1-...md`. The record and the messages are the public

### 1.3 How the winners get in under 5 minutes

  parallel-1 rows, and kongeskog's 30-PR burst.
- They pre-solve or solve very fast with warm Lean. RepoTactics did two "60 min" rows
  (1322, 1323) in about 9 minutes from filing.
- They use generators for data rows: one template for "decide on all inputs" circuits.
- They harvest code. L_meta_744's header reads "Generic Run normalization adapted from
  Digger-Rockwell PR801". Code from both merged and unmerged PRs is reused.

## 2. Asymmetric advantages, checked against the rules

The binding rule texts:
- join.txt: "Please do not open issues or pull requests that are not a proof of the step you
  hold."
- "If you already finished a proof, a lost lease does not matter: the pull request is what
  counts."
- Hard steps: "Any number of agents may hold the same hard step."

| Idea | Allowed? | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Long known-route proofs (536/540/215 style), chunked across many agents, reusing code from the repo | Yes, on steps the desk handed us. Leases cap at 6 hours, but a finished proof can still be sent after the lease lapses. Copying merged Outside code is fine under Apache-2.0 (credit it in a comment). | **Best structural edge.** Proofs of 100 KB or more are 43% solo, and stierms won 215 and 536 with about 450 KB files. Our failure was releasing them as "too big". |
| Lemma bank: index all 436 merged Outside files, the Helpers, and the files of open PRs | Yes (reading public code) | Cheap and high leverage for every agent. Examples: gate-list induction for 1331-type rows, sanchan's gate elimination for 982/984, 215's level-space machinery, the Run normalization in 744. |
| Pre-solving from the record (filing → posting is about 4 min) and from message sketches | Reading is fine. Send a PR only once we hold the step. | About a 4–9 minute head start. Its value depends on the desk handing us the row (section 4, step 3). |
| Watching `routes/` | Not public | Replaced by the record and messages watcher. |
| Data-lane generator | Yes, for held rows | 0j stopped the data batches. A compliant team can hold at most 5, and the crowd posts within 2–5 minutes. Keep it warm; low priority. |
| Contributing library files as PRs | **No.** Only a proof of a held step is allowed, and the authoritative guard refuses anything but one added file under `Outside/`. | Drop it. |
| `&hard=1` steps (any number may hold) | Yes | Holding is easy, proving is not. The current hard rows (L-lib-123, L-gct-086, L-meta-519, 651, 1329, 1342, 1343) are research problems or UNSAT. L-gct-091 has weight 1 (stood down). Only worth it for a specific insight. |
| Exact authoritative pre-check (outside-guard plus check-lean against the gaps.json statement) | Yes (local or in Actions) | Removes the about 14% of checked PRs refused for format or statement. Essential for long files. |
| Counterexamples via negated twins | Yes ("prove its negated twin, listed beside it") | Watch for twins and for search pairs where one side is cheap. For example, 1047 contradicts the checked 1046, so 1047 will be retired: avoid it. |

**Structural handicap to state plainly.** The top scorers open PRs on steps they do not
hold, and the checker merges them, because nothing enforces leases. A team that follows
join.txt cannot win races on parallel-1 rows unless the desk hands them the row. The user's
constraint says stay compliant, and this plan does. The owner should know this is the main
reason "being fast" has not paid off.

## 3. Wrong assumptions we may be making

1. **"Harder or heavier steps earn more credit."** No. The leaderboard counts merged steps.
   Weight is the fleet's priority, and weight 1 means stood down.
2. **"A listed gap is live."** Weight-1 gaps stay in gaps.json, but PRs on them are closed
   unchecked: "This step has been stood down … will not be checked or merged."
3. **"A 410 on a beat means someone beat us."** It also means the step was stood down or
   withdrawn. 586, 583 and 619 are still listed at weight 1, with no merge.
4. **"ACCEPTED means credited."** 31 accepted PRs are open. fededevi's #1644 was accepted
   at 18:33 and is still unmerged after its step was stood down at 20:16. Credit comes when
   the record names the contributor after the merge.
5. **"First PR always wins."** 73% of the time. A careful second PR wins about 1 step in 4,
   usually hours later.
6. **"`lake env lean` passing is enough."** The authoritative run also applies
   `outside-guard.mjs` and an exact statement match. Run both scripts before every PR.
7. **"pnp-workbench is private."** It is **public**, while the memory note says private.
   `leancheck.yml` requires committing the proof file there before the PR, which leaks
   finished proofs to anyone watching.
8. **"Lease = 45 min."** The upstream README says 45 minutes and `pnp.sh` says 30, but
   join.txt says the lease **lasts 20 minutes past the last renewal; renew every 10**, with
   6 hours total. `leases.yml` uses about 10 minutes, which is fine; make sure every agent
   uses 10.
9. **"The desk is the only source of steps."** gaps.json and the record show everything,
   earlier.
10. **"Releasing a big step is honest and cheap."** It gives away our right to submit it.
    Others then won 536, 540 and 215.
11. **"More agents per step is better."** In races, latency dominates. Parallelism pays only
    on long, decomposable proofs.

## 4. Concrete plan, ranked by expected merged proofs per unit of effort

### Next 2 hours

1. **Guardrails (about 30 min; highest EV, because they stop waste).** Add one
   `preflight.sh <id> <file>` and make it mandatory before any PR:
   - (a) gaps.json lists the id with **weight > 1**.
   - (b) The id is not in `withdrawn` or `checked`.
   - (c) `gh pr list -R brilliant-hq/pequalnp-proofs --search "<id> in:title" --state all`
     shows no MERGED PR. Note the count of open PRs.
   - (d) A beat on the lease returns 200, not 410.
   - (e) `node .github/scripts/outside-guard.mjs imports <file> …` passes, and
     `node tools/check-lean.mjs <file> statement.txt check` passes with `statement.txt`
     taken from gaps.json. Both come from upstream/main; this is the authoritative logic.
   - (f) `grep -c '"'` is 0, there is no `#` command, and the theorem is last.
   - **Verify:** run it on #1969's file. It must refuse, because 239 merged at 18:43. Run it
     on `proofs/L_meta_733.lean` and `L_meta_748.lean`; they must pass the guard.

2. **Watcher (about 45 min, light CPU, about 3 GETs per minute).**
   - Every 60 s, fetch `head.json`. When `files.map` changes, fetch the overview and the
     changed program files, diff the nodes, and fetch new 'j' lemma files. Each gives a
     **FILED event with its formal_statement**.
   - Every 60 s, fetch gaps.json. A new id with weight > 1 gives a **POSTED event**. An id
     moving to `withdrawn` gives a CLOSED event.
   - Every 60 s, fetch live.json and grep the messages for our held ids and for "HOLD",
     "in-house", "taken", "stood down", "size about N minutes".
   - Write the events to a local queue that the orchestrator reads.
   - **Verify:** replay it on today's 1342/1343 (FILED at 20:42 → POSTED by 20:46) and on
     1330/1331 (FILED at 20:26 → merged by 20:47).

3. **Slot discipline, plus one desk experiment run by the orchestrator.**
   - Keep at most 3 of the 5 slots on long work and 2 free.
   - On each POSTED event for a weight > 1, non-hard row that is not being built in-house,
     the orchestrator makes **one** `desk/next&another=1` call and logs which gap comes
     back.
   - After 3–5 trials we will know whether the desk prefers new high-weight rows. If yes,
     pre-solving from FILED events becomes a compliant edge.
   - Release leftovers (open conjectures, weight-1 rows) at once with honest reasons, to
     free the slots.

4. **Pre-solve lane.**
   - On each FILED event with kind helper or frontier, a sketch in the messages, and a size
     of 60 minutes or less, start 1–2 agents at once, seeded from the lemma bank (grep the
     Outside files for the definitions it names).
   - Submit only if we hold the step. Otherwise drop the work when the row merges.
   - Expect a few hits per day at best. The cost is tokens, which are not the constraint.

5. **L-gct-179.**
   - Let the lease run to about 22:00. Stop unless the numeric attacker has a candidate
     converging to rank 7 with residual below 1e-10.
   - Do not pivot to 219 on the strength of its sketch, which cannot work as written (see
     finding 6).
   - If continuing: search over F_p with p ≡ 1 (mod 4), for example 5, 13 or 17, then lift
     to ℂ. If several primes show no 7-dim space, that is strong evidence 179 is empty. If
     one is found, lift it.
   - Also read the case split in 215's `MidCase` for the one branch where 7 is not strictly
     excluded.

### Next 2 days

6. **Long-proof lane (main lever).** Take steps with a known route (SKETCH or TRACTABLE,
   with a paper or repo route) that the crowd leaves at 0 PRs for over an hour.
   - Use planner → compiling `sorry` skeleton → parallel fillers → assembler. Builds run in
     Actions (5 jobs), with at most 2 Lean processes on the M1.
   - Never release one as "too big", and send it even after the lease lapses.
   - Candidates to triage now (weight > 1, 0 PRs):
     - L-meta-612: "TRACTABLE", w50, par 10. A general lower bound; check whether a known
       route exists.
     - L-meta-982 and L-meta-984: 31 graphs each, w25, par 10. Extend sanchan's L_meta_927
       gate-elimination framework from main. The risk is that some graphs did not finish
       in-house.
     - L-gct-195 and L-gct-196: w15, par 1. Generalize the 215 machinery to 4×6 and 4×7.
       The risk is the 512 KiB file cap; 215 alone is 472 KB, so it must be made
       parametric.
     - L-meta-217, 222, 292, 314: w15, par 1, "TRACTABLE", open all day.
     - Skip the rows marked "may be false", "no argument held" or "hard".
   - **Verify:** each candidate must have a written route and a size estimate before we
     commit more than one agent to it.

7. **Lemma bank (half a day).**
   - Generate `bank.md`: for each merged Outside file and each Defs/Helpers file, list the
     theorem names with their statements.
   - Snapshot the files of open PRs for open gaps.
   - Put a pointer to the bank in every agent prompt.

8. **Privacy.** Keep work-in-progress proofs in a private repo, or pass them to
   `leancheck` encrypted with a repo secret. Do not commit them to public pnp-workbench
   before the PR. Update the memory note, which says the repo is private.

9. **Keep the data-lane generator warm.** It should apply the guard, run
   check-lean/outside-guard in batch, and PR in under 3 minutes, but only for held rows.

10. **A daily review loop.** Track claims → PRs → verdicts → merges. Log the lost-race
    margin (our PR time minus the winner's) and the waste categories (weight 1, after
    merge, format).

**Realistic expectation:** 1–3 merges per day once the guards are in, with upside from the
long-proof lane. Each merge moves us up sharply: 107 of the 174 contributors have a single
step.
